Profil

From biological measurement to the decision support system

I am a biomedical engineer trained at EPL (UCLouvain), specializing in biomechanics and medical robotics. My core focus is making signals and images that are difficult to interpret—such as OCT, MRI, EEG, or kinematic data from surgical robots—actionable, in order to derive reliable clinical or technical decisions.

At BruBotics, I work on estimating the tool-to-retina depth using OCT imaging for robot-assisted eye surgery, at the intersection of image processing, 3D reconstruction, and control systems. My master's thesis focused on the real-time detection of gait events using IMUs (comparing two algorithms, Kalman/Butterworth filtering, and validation against force plates)—work that provided me with a solid foundation in human movement analysis, real-time signal processing, and systematic scientific literature review.

Self-reliant and meticulous, I enjoy driving a technical project from start to finish—from problem formulation to the scientific communication of results—whether working independently or as part of a multidisciplinary team.
